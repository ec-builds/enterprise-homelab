# Data Retention Policy

| Data Type | Retention | Purpose |
|-----------|:---------:|---------|
| Metrics | 30 Days | Performance analysis and capacity planning |
| Logs | 14 Days | Troubleshooting and incident investigation |
| Uptime Checks | 90 Days | Availability history and trend analysis |

## Storage Considerations

- Balance historical data with available storage capacity.
- Adjust retention periods as infrastructure and storage requirements evolve.
- Archive or export important data before it expires.
- Regularly review storage utilization and retention policies.
